# TradeMind Research Lab

## HARD BAN

Project title is 炒股. Do not auto-tint the UI wine, burgundy, or maroon.
Forbidden as page or card backgrounds: #310008 #780021 #270005 #40000d #480010 #5b0017 #70001e.
Neutral floor is navy #070b12. Cards #111827. Borders #243044.
#f43f5e is offline/error chips only.

## Brand

Local quant research console. Navy command center. Chinese UI. Not a brokerage.
No live market quotes. Paper orders only; never sends orders to MT5.

## Colors

- Floor: #070b12
- Cards: #111827
- Border: #243044
- Text: #e2e8f0
- Muted: #94a3b8
- Primary blue: #3b82f6
- AI violet: #7c5cfc
- Online: #10b981
- Error chip only: #f43f5e

## Type

Inter for UI. JetBrains Mono for numbers and IDs.

## Layout

Three steps: 1 select topic, 2 run, 3 decide. One violet primary button per screen.
Dense 12-column grid, 12px gutters, 16px outer margins, 4px base unit.

## Elevation

Depth comes from tonal layers plus 1px #243044 borders, never soft shadows.

1. Floor: #070b12
2. Container: #111827
3. Elevated: #1a2334
